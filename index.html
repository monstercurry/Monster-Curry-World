Curry Personality Test

A lightweight prototype that turns a few simple decisions into a curry personality. It is intentionally brand-neutral, grayscale, and focused on the flow of answering, scoring, and revealing a result.

Flow Intro, 5 questions, result
Visual style Placeholder art and monochrome UI
Scoring Simple buckets from answer choices
Output Curry personality with short facts

The prototype deliberately keeps the content generic so the workflow can be reviewed without brand lock-in or final illustration work.
